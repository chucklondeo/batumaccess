About Batum

R&D and manufacturing company focused on low-voltage servo access control

Batum develops drive control, motion control and hardware manufacturing capability for vehicle and pedestrian access systems. Products cover high-speed barrier gates, direct-drive pedestrian gates, platform screen door control, door operators and safety accessories. Safety is the first design objective.
